# How to Use This Swipe File

Each hook follows this format:
- **Formula Name** — What it does
- **Template** — Fill in the [BRACKETS] with your specifics
- **Examples** — Real-world applications

**Pro tip:** Don't just copy — adapt. The best hooks feel native to your voice and audience.

# BONUS: Hook Stacking Formulas

**The Triple Stack:** Combine curiosity + credibility + urgency
> "After 10 years in sales (credibility), I discovered the one question that doubles close rates (curiosity). And I'm only sharing it this week (urgency)."

**The Contrast Stack:** Combine pain point + unexpected solution
> "Tired of posting every day with no results? (pain) The top creators I know post twice a week. (contrast)"

**The Story Stack:** Combine transformation + specific result + framework
> "I went from $0 to $50K months (transformation) in 6 months (specific). Here's my 4-part framework (actionable)."
