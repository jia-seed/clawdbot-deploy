## 📊 Analytics & Tracking

### 1. Google Analytics 4
**What it does:** Full website analytics—traffic sources, user behavior, conversions, audience demographics  
**Free tier:** Unlimited (seriously)  
**Setup difficulty:** Medium  
**Notes:** Industry standard. Steep learning curve but incredibly powerful. Worth mastering.

### 2. Plausible Analytics (Self-Hosted)
**What it does:** Privacy-focused, lightweight analytics. Simple dashboard, no cookies  
**Free tier:** Free if self-hosted on your own server  
**Setup difficulty:** Hard (requires server)  
**Notes:** Cloud version is paid, but the open-source self-hosted version is completely free.

### 3. Umami
**What it does:** Privacy-first, open-source Google Analytics alternative  
**Free tier:** Unlimited self-hosted; cloud free tier: 10K events/month  
**Setup difficulty:** Medium (self-hosted) / Easy (cloud)  
**Notes:** Beautiful UI, GDPR-compliant by design. No cookies needed.

### 4. Hotjar
**What it does:** Heatmaps, session recordings, user feedback polls  
**Free tier:** 35 daily sessions, unlimited heatmaps  
**Setup difficulty:** Easy  
**Notes:** See exactly where users click, scroll, and rage-click. Essential for conversion optimization.

### 5. Microsoft Clarity
**What it does:** Heatmaps and session recordings  
**Free tier:** Unlimited (completely free)  
**Setup difficulty:** Easy  
**Notes:** Microsoft's answer to Hotjar. No limits whatsoever. Excellent free alternative.

### 6. Google Search Console
**What it does:** Track search performance, indexing issues, keyword rankings  
**Free tier:** Unlimited  
**Setup difficulty:** Easy  
**Notes:** Non-negotiable for SEO. Shows you exactly what queries bring traffic.

### 7. Mixpanel
**What it does:** Product analytics, user journey tracking, funnel analysis  
**Free tier:** 20M events/month  
**Setup difficulty:** Medium  
**Notes:** More powerful than GA4 for product analytics. Generous free tier.

### 8. PostHog
**What it does:** Product analytics, session recording, feature flags, A/B testing  
**Free tier:** 1M events/month, 5K session recordings  
**Setup difficulty:** Medium  
**Notes:** All-in-one product analytics. Open-source. Incredibly feature-rich for free.

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Start small.** Pick ONE tool per category. Don't overcomplicate.

2. **Master before adding.** Use a tool for 30 days before adding another.

3. **Connect everything.** Use Make or Zapier to connect tools—that's where magic happens.

4. **Self-host when ready.** Tools like n8n, PostHog, and Umami become unlimited when self-hosted.

5. **Watch limits.** Set calendar reminders when approaching free tier limits.

6. **Export regularly.** Always maintain data portability. Export contacts, content, analytics monthly.

7. **Upgrade strategically.** When one tool becomes a bottleneck, upgrade that ONE tool. Don't upgrade everything at once.
